We’ve officially reached a fever pitch in the Endeavor. *Two* posts in a day, wow. I left you all on a cliffhanger in [my last post](/posts/practice-my-soldering) and I needed to make it right. 

This is one of those tasks that is conveniently self-documenting, so I’ll be keeping my comments brief. I professed my love for live remix videos when discussing the [history of my studio](/posts/set-up-my-studio), but there's a broader landscape of gear videos of which I am also a frequent viewer. The keen observer might have noticed that many of the effects on my pedalboard were at one time or another featured in videos by [Knobs](https://www.youtube.com/c/Knobs), a channel which answers the question of what it would look like if Wes Anderson filmed equipment demos. 

Today, I’m adding an entry to the storied history of gear videos, though I make no claim on clearing the aesthetic bar set by Knobs. It features the DirtyWave M8 previously used for [URLegy](https://urlegy.com), as well as the gizmo I just finished soldering: a [3TrinsRGB+1](https://gieskes.nl/instruments/?file=3TrinsRGB1) video synthesizer by gieskes. I always thought the two would make for quite the cool, compact pair and wanted to test my theory.
