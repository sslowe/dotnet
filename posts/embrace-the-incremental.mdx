With my birthday sojourn firmly in the rearview, the time has arrived to pick up the pace on the Endeavor once more. If I’m to put a bow on this thing by the month’s end, the math works out to essentially a post-per-weekday rate. As always, my sights are set preposterously high. Brush up on all my misplaced confidence from the start of series [here](/posts/document-everything).

I’ll go ahead and warn you up top that I’m bringing you my second ponderous, philosophical post in a row. I usually do my best to space them out better, but I promise I’ll at least try to be humorous about it. To make matters even worse, this is the closest I’m ever going to get to discussing fitness as a topic. 

## (Not a) Fitness Blog

Since the very inception of the list, there has been a certain appeal to low-hanging fruit. Heck, at one point “Pluck a low-hanging fruit” was an actual line item as a reminder to not hastily dismiss ideas that might initially seem *too* obvious. There are far too many examples of great works of art or invention happening in a burst of inspiration to embrace the opposing stance that only difficult things are worthwhile. The problem with including it on the list is that it’s more of an means to an end rather than an end in itself, so completing it would entail conjuring a frivolous extracurricular project or writing a second blog post about how I took the easy way out on some other task. Riveting stuff, indeed.

One particular brand of low-hanging fruit that I’ve done my best to avoid is putting things on the list that I was just going to do regardless. I mentioned in my first post that I got married this year, and in the lead-up to my wedding day, I began the first concerted effort of my adult life to get in shape. Growing up, I was always someone who only enjoyed exercise within the context of sports, so as my high school tennis days morphed into intramural basketball in college morphed into not much to speak of, there weren’t any non-sporting activities to take its place.

In my quest for efficiency (read: laziness), I’ve always been on the hunt for the optimal all-in-one workout. Just ask anyone whose inquisitive gaze settled on the Maxi Climber in my college bedroom. This time, at least, I landed on something with a little more street cred and incorporated a rowing machine session into my daily routine. There’s really not anything high-minded to unpack about my exercise regimen. In my [MindWalk post](/posts/engineer-an-ai-system), I discussed how my Router co-founder, Scott, has always been a proponent of using his daily training for reflection and ideation. Me? I watch television.

What I *have* spent a lot of time thinking about is the way the progress I’ve made on my own fitness compares and contrasts to the progress I’ve made on the Endeavor, chiefly the gradual consistency of the former and the fits and starts of most of my work on the latter. My creative process tends to consist of long periods of exploration where I assimilate new information into my model of some problem followed by periods of deep work, but there’s just no substitute for daily practice. It’s a lesson that anyone who picked up an instrument in their childhood learned at one point, but it’s one that’s easy to forget in the hectic vortex of adulthood. Enough has been said on the relationship between discipline and inspiration that I don’t feel the need to wade into those waters, but suffice it to say I want to be better about embracing the discipline portion of the equation.

The side effect of forcing myself into a near-daily posting timeframe is that writing is now an inescapable part of my day for the remainder of the Endeavor, and I intend to keep that up after its conclusion, albeit not solely for the purpose of these posts. I’ve also gotten back into the habit of playing music every day, even if I can hear my old drum teacher’s voice in my head lecturing me on the difference between playing and practicing. Hey, it’s a start. The last box I’d like to check is to add a daily songwriting practice as part of my routine, but that’s the thing I’ve been most intimidated at attempting in a time-boxed manner. <span className="text-[#77FFE4] text-sm">[As a matter of fact, I’m going to go do that as soon as I finish writing this post, otherwise I couldn’t very well cross the task off the list, could I?]</span>

With this post, we’ve officially made it to the other side of my post-29 reverie. I promise next time I’ll be back with something more exciting to look at than another one of my [cartoons](/posts/draw-some-cartoons). if nothing else.
