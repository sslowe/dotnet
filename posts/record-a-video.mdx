It's officially crunch time in the Endeavor, and I’m going to be churning out list items at a fever pitch until the end is in sight. If, unlike me, you have time to spare, you can catch up from the start of my efforts with the [first post in the series](/posts/document-everything).

This is one of those tasks that is conveniently self-documenting, so I’ll be keeping my comments brief. I professed my love for live remix videos when discussing the [history of my studio](/posts/set-up-my-studio), but there's a much broader landscape of gear videos of which I am also a frequent consumer. It’s a genre that stokes and satiates my ceaseless yearning for new gizmos in equal measure. The keen observer might have noticed in the photos from that post that many of the effects on my pedalboard were at one time or another featured in videos by [Knobs](https://www.youtube.com/c/Knobs), a channel which answers the question of what it would look like if Wes Anderson filmed equipment demos. 

Today, I’m adding an entry of my own to the storied history of gear videos, though I make no claims on clearing the aesthetic bar set by Knobs. It features the tabletop, “sound lab” half of my studio, specifically the DirtyWave M8 previously used for [URLegy](https://urlegy.com), a Vermona DRM1 drum machine, the MicroMonsta 2 synthesizer by Audiothingies, and my patch bay pedalboard.
