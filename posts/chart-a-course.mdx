Well, well, well... After spending the start of last week oscillating between panic at the amount of work left in the Endeavor and serenity that there was simply no way to get it done on time, I decided to take a step back from my list in the final run-up to my birthday to enjoy the waning days of 28. So, am I 29 now? Yes. Am I calling it quits over something as silly as an incongruous title? Not a chance. Head back to [my first post](/posts/document-everything) in the series if you’d like to catch up on the tasks I did manage to get done in time.

While ~~mother~~ editor has caught flak in the past on the blogramo for various childhood grudges (like [putting the kibosh](/posts/build-an-audience) on my online friendships), today I want to give her some well-deserved flowers for being the trailblazer she is. You see, ~~moother~~ editor has never felt that the concept of her birth*day* was sufficient in any regard. No, it’s her whole birth*week* that warrants recognition, and in recent years, even that has been found lacking. So I’m taking a page from her book, and we’re celebrating my 29th the whole month of September, which means I haven’t missed my deadline at all. <span className="text-[#77FFE4] text-sm">[Look, if Augustus can call dibs on a whole month, so can I.]</span>

“Chart a course” was originally intended to be my final task and has undergone several rounds of revision reflecting my changing thoughts on what it is I hope to achieve in crossing it off the list, from the mundane (“Sum it all up”) to the distillatory (“Identify pillars"). In the time I had to reflect on the Endeavor thus far, I’ve realized that this midpoint in the list is a prime opportunity to calibrate my intentions for the remainder of the series and, more broadly, where I hope to be by age 30. After all, if 29 got me in this much of a tizzy, I can only imagine what 30 is going to do.

Back at the outset of this Endeavor, I purposefully went broad in constructing the list, including every passing fancy I could call to mind with the goal of discovering the things that warranted further exploration. And in that regard, it’s been a great success. I certainly didn’t expect I would be [conceptualizing](/posts/develop-a-game) and [designing](/posts/fabricate-a-product) a card game from scratch, but that’s where my interests led. It’s also been massively helpful in developing a body of work online in short order. At the end of the day, however, no singular task is liable to move the needle much on its own. <span className="text-[#77FFE4] text-sm">[Except for when I finally finish my Survivor audition tape, that is.]</span>

With that in mind, I’ve realized that the best use of the remaining tasks is to consolidate my efforts around a smaller set of creative goals for the upcoming year and atomize the preliminary work in each of those directions. Of course, I still have overly ambitious ideas about what I’ll be able to achieve by my next birthday. That’s just the sacramo way. At least I’m getting an early start this time. The vast majority of the list has been revised at least once at this point, but this time around, I modified half of the remaining items in one fell swoop to focus on three main areas: music, writing, and games. <span className="text-[#77FFE4] text-sm">[Given the fact that I’ve never shared the list in its entirety, I could have just pretended that this was the plan all along, but then I wouldn’t get to cross something off the list by writing about it.]</span>

There’s a part of me that’s hesitant to let go of my original, broader vision of the Endeavor. By my own admission, I probably wouldn’t have stumbled upon the idea for *Priority Boarding* without giving myself the space to discover it. One list item that I’ve held onto since the first draft that finally hit the chopping block was “Prototype hardware”. It was the main reason I bashed my head against that synthesizer kit for [soldering practice](/posts/practice-my-soldering) to begin with. Like I’ve [mentioned previously](/posts/set-up-my-studio), I’m a huge nerd about music tech, and I thought it could be fun to play around with circuit bending or electro-acoustic instruments. But that was ambitious back when I thought I’d be able to dedicate an entire week to just that task, let alone the borrowed time I’m currently operating on. There’s nothing to say that I can’t return to those kind of experiments outside the bounds of the Endeavor, though. I could even write *more* blog posts about it. 

I understand if you have questions, so let me end by addressing them. First, did I write an entire post just to summarize changes to a list that I’m still not sharing the rest of? Yes. Second, have I overused rhetorical questions as a literary device in this post? Also yes, though I can’t say that I’m all that sorry for either.
